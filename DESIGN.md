# I Believe Tools

The site is a stone-yard specification sheet, not a consumer product page.

## Color

- Limestone `#e6dccb` and paper `#f4efe6` carry the page.
- Ink `#1c1612` is the text and the solid button.
- Slag `#161311` is used for the market section and table headers.
- Copper `#c84b24` is the only accent. It is the kerf: a 2px cut line, large italic indexes, and focus rings. It is not a fill for body text.
- Chip `#d5e3e6` is secondary text on slag.

## Type

- English, German, and Italian: Fraunces for headlines, Barlow for text, Barlow Condensed for navigation, labels, and specification tables.
- Chinese: Noto Serif SC and Noto Sans SC, with Barlow Condensed kept for Latin specification numbers.
- Russian: Source Serif 4 and Golos Text.
- Arabic: Amiri and IBM Plex Sans Arabic. The locale layout sets `dir="rtl"`. Layout uses logical properties. The kerf line grows from the inline start.

## Motion

One entrance: the copper line draws across, then the headline rises. Product and material rows grow the same line on hover. `prefers-reduced-motion` removes both.

## Structure

Saw blades lead. Cup wheels and core bits are a second row. Markets describe the cut common in Europe, India, Central Asia, and the Middle East. Factory facts stay inside the brochure: founded 2014, 3,000 m², more than 2 million pieces a year, ISO 9001:2000, German MPA.
